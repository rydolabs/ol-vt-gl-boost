## 2. Technical reason for R79 and its safety contract

R78 isolated a SwiftShader-specific synchronous stall in `src/gl/backend.js`: unconditional per-frame `gl.getError()` synchronously waited for queued rasterization in `endFrame()`, causing huge **main-thread renderSync submission** times. Omitting the poll reduced time, **not proven GPU draw cost or FPS**.

R79 adds `glErrorCheckMode: 'strict' | 'nonblocking'`:
- Direct `WebGL1Backend` defaults to `strict`, preserving previous behavior.
- **Opt-in `BoostMap`** defaults to `nonblocking`; `boostOptions.backendOptions.glErrorCheckMode:'strict'` is explicit rollback.
- `nonblocking` calls `gl.flush()` but omits per-frame `gl.getError()`. Its status records `glErrorChecked:false` and `glError:null` (**unchecked is NOT equivalent to WebGL NO_ERROR**). An explicit `checkGlError()` outside measured paths is available.
- Forced error in **strict** mode and `WEBGL_lose_context` context loss in nonblocking mode fall back to Stock OL in tests.
- **Open risk:** arbitrary non-context-loss GL errors are no longer caught synchronously each frame by nonblocking mode. Forced strict-mode error recovery and context-loss recovery do **not** prove the same recovery for all asynchronous errors. Decide/document explicit risk acceptance or implement a safe diagnostic policy and retest.

R76 drop-in excludes unsupported conditional/text/icon/expression/hit-detection/mixed-layer cases by fail-closed Stock OL fallback. The R77 workload is **not** the full OpenLayers official WebGL vector tile demo; it uses a controlled literal-style set and 32 SHA-distinct local MapLibre/OpenMapTiles MVT PBFs with identical styles/view.

| Metric | Stock OL | R79 Boost |
|---|---:|---:|
| P50 rAF gap | 31.1ms | 31.1ms |

**Key conclusion:** CPU submission is clearly faster, but **rAF scheduling cadence was effectively equal** (~32Hz both). No evidence here of an RTX tail-latency regression; no evidence of a measured rAF-frequency win either.

#### R81 real-GPU visual, recovery and 60-second soak

- Stock-vs-Boost post-pan screenshot mismatch: **0.93%** of pixels for 8 layers, **4.06%** for 32; 32-layer mean absolute RGB channel difference **0.13/255**. Threshold oracle passed, but this is **NOT pixel-for-pixel Stock OL equivalence**. Strict-vs-nonblocking Boost screenshots were byte-for-byte identical in earlier tests.
- Explicit forced GL error under `strict` and simulated WebGL context loss under `nonblocking` each restored Stock OL: **PASS**.
- Continuous 32-layer Boost for **60.02s**, 1,919 pan iterations; rAF p95 **32ms**, `renderSync` p50 **0.9ms**, post-pan GL check **NO_ERROR**, retained all 32 tiles and 2 GL programs. JS heap rose **4,845,781 bytes** during the trial, not conclusive leakage.
- Driver reported NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti with driver **610.47**, total GPU memory **16,311MiB**. `nvidia-smi` captured **50 samples** of **GPU-global** used VRAM: **399–671MiB** (first **453**, last **400MiB**); **not Boost-process-specific VRAM**. A successful short trial cannot rule out a long-term VRAM leak.
